## Tiers

| Tier | Band | Benefits | Never includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Friend | < $500 | name in release notes thanks | — |
| Supporter | $500–5k | logo on website sponsors page | — |
| Lab sponsor | $5k–25k (cash or kind) | named in ASSETS.md + annual report; lab-hardware donations logged, no placement conditions on blessed SKUs | SKU blessing by preference |
| CI/compute sponsor | credits | named on CI page; credits don't change repro-parity (ch.09 §2) or retention policy | gate skip |
| Security-review sponsor | $10k+ | funds pentest/bounty-equivalent (ch.11 §18 cat. 5); scope set by Security + Arch | early access to findings; sponsor never previews before the advisory clock |

## Declined regardless of amount (ch.11 §23, kind-final-logged)

- Feature-bounty-for-merge — review and gates are not for sale.
- Gate-skipping ("fund us to bless carrier X by Friday") — blessed means measured (ch.07 §11).
- Non-goal reversal (GMS/L1 bundling) — bar named, not moved (ADR-004).
- Embargo-early-access payments — findings reach advisory on the same clock for everyone.
